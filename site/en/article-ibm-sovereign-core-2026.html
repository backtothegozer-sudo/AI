In its official Think 2026 announcement on 5 May 2026, IBM confirmed general availability of IBM Sovereign Core. The signal is material: beyond model access, IBM is packaging an operational layer to run AI workloads under local control with continuous governance evidence.

1. What is officially announced

IBM positions IBM Sovereign Core as an operational foundation for making digital sovereignty executable. The approach targets organizations that need to run sensitive workloads with data residency, customer-managed control planes, and enforceable compliance policies over time.

2. Why this matters for sovereign AI execution

The announcement moves from strategy language to operational delivery: sovereign AI is not only about model selection, but about verifiable control of operations, access boundaries, and governance evidence. General availability is the key execution milestone.

3. Operational reading for enterprises

For IT, data, cyber, and compliance teams, the immediate step is to map critical AI workloads, define evidence obligations, and formalize local-control requirements per entity and jurisdiction before full production rollout.

For Belgium, France, and Odoo Enterprise rollouts, the practical takeaway is to align AI features, ERP workflows, document handling, and inference governance before scaling assistant or agent use cases into production.
