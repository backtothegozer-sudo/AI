OpenAI x Samsung: ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex go company-wide

Article created on 25 June 2026 · Publication analyzed: 21 June 2026 · Source: OpenAI

Share inLinkedIn XX fFacebook

On June 21, 2026, OpenAI announced that Samsung Electronics is deploying ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex at scale: all Samsung employees in Korea get access, along with all global employees in the Device eXperience division. This is a strong signal for AI Belgium, AI France, Odoo Belgium, Odoo France, and Odoo Enterprise: enterprise AI is moving from targeted pilots to a cross-functional work platform.

1. What is officially being announced

OpenAI describes this as one of its largest enterprise launches to date. Samsung plans to use ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex across technical and non-technical functions: software development, marketing, R&D, manufacturing, documentation, information analysis, and problem-solving. The key message is clear: Codex is no longer confined to pure coding tasks; it is also becoming an accelerator for internal workflows, business tools, and automation.

2. Why this matters for enterprise AI strategy

The announcement also foregrounds enterprise governance layers: data protection, user and access management, security controls, and alignment with internal policy. For Odoo Belgium, Odoo France, and Odoo Enterprise programs, that is the central issue: value does not appear when AI stays at the edge of the organization, but when it enters real operations without breaking security, compliance, or role control.

3. Operational takeaway for organizations in Belgium and France

Company-wide adoption means moving beyond the idea of "an assistant for a few experts." Teams need to define who gets access to which use cases, which connectors are approved, what content can be generated, how automations are logged, and where human approvals remain mandatory. In systems that combine ERP, support, purchasing, finance, and documentation, the challenge is not only individual productivity but keeping AI workflows coherent with business governance.

Prioritize a role-based rollout: use cases, access rules, approved data, human validation points, and action logging before opening AI company-wide.
