NAVER x NVIDIA: Korea pushes sovereign AI into AI-factory scale

Article created on 9 June 2026 · Publication analyzed: 7 June 2026 · Source: NVIDIA Newsroom

Share inLinkedIn XX fFacebook

The official announcement published on 7 June 2026 by NVIDIA puts Korea on an industrial sovereign-AI trajectory. NAVER plans to expand infrastructure from an initial 55 MW toward gigawatt scale to run AI factories serving enterprises, industry, and government.

1. What is actually new here

This goes beyond a standard technology partnership. It marks a scale-up of sovereign AI from localized model hosting into national production capacity, where training, inference, and AI services are treated as strategic industrial infrastructure.

The key point is the combination of infrastructure, regional models, and execution. NAVER plans to use the NVIDIA stack to advance HyperCLOVA X, agentic services, and future AI factories on top of a platform positioned for trusted and sovereignty-aware deployment.

2. Why this is a strong sovereign-AI signal

The move from 55 MW toward a gigawatt ambition shows that sovereign AI is now measured in continuous production capacity. The issue is no longer just where data sits, but whether a country or operator has enough compute, orchestration control, and operational autonomy to run real workloads at scale.

For Europe, and especially for Belgium and France, the signal is clear: meaningful sovereign-AI positioning requires an end-to-end chain, from data center capacity to specialized models and the business services that actually depend on them.

3. Practical reading for Odoo Enterprise and business teams

Odoo Belgium, Odoo France, and Odoo Enterprise roadmaps can read this as an architecture lesson. Once AI becomes a production service, teams need to decide early which ERP workflows, documents, agents, and critical datasets must remain inside a tightly governed execution zone.

The better approach is to separate commoditized productivity use cases from sensitive processes that require more control over compute, model behavior, and observability. That is what makes a credible mix possible across global cloud, local cloud, and dedicated sovereign infrastructure.
