Enterprise AI: measuring end-to-end workflow time

Article created November 28, 2026 · Study published November 23, 2026 · Source Anthropic

In a study published on November 23, Anthropic drew on 66 interviews across 58 of its enterprise customers in the United States. Respondents described tasks that sped up with AI while review, sales or decision-making still had to keep pace. The sample is nonrepresentative, but it suggests a question teams can test in their own operations: when one task speeds up, does the case finish sooner?

1. Agree with the business on when the workflow ends

For an invoice processing pilot in Odoo Enterprise, measure turnaround from receipt to accounting approval. In both the current and AI-assisted workflows, also record active processing time, time spent waiting for review and rework. Compare cases of similar complexity and count exceptions routed back to manual processing.

2. What this means for Belgian and French companies

An SME can track one type of invoice and check whether the approval queue grows as preparation speeds up. A mid-market company operating in Belgium and France can break down results by entity and approval workflow. In both cases, accounting and IT should agree on errors that would prevent rollout, such as an incorrectly identified supplier or an invoice recorded twice.

3. Underside’s analysis of where AI and approval fit

An agent can prepare a draft in the ERP and use retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) to consult procedures within its document permissions. Keep human approval before posting and test that the agent’s identity cannot bypass it. When comparing local and cloud execution, check where documents pass through, where logs are stored and who can access them. These criteria help assess control over the workflow and its data.

4. Set acceptance criteria before testing

Set targets for median and 90th-percentile turnaround and a threshold for rework. Track cases still waiting at the end of the test. If preparation speeds up and the review queue grows, adjust review capacity or narrow the scope before expanding automation.

Before expanding the pilot, have the workflow owner review the results and return a case to manual processing with its history intact.
