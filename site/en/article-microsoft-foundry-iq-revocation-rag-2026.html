Document RAG: measure how long it takes to revoke access

Article created on November 25, 2026 · Source published on November 24, 2026 · Microsoft

In its November 24 Ignite roundup, Microsoft says the SharePoint indexed knowledge source for Foundry IQ is now generally available. Its documentation describes how search results are filtered at query time using the user’s identity and indexed permissions. How quickly those permissions update depends on the indexer’s processing.

1. Measure the delay with a test folder

Create a mock folder in a test environment and add a unique phrase. Grant two accounts access and verify that both can retrieve the phrase and its citation. Then revoke one account’s access and record when you made the change. Repeat the search and conversation at regular intervals with both accounts. Record the last successful retrieval and the first sustained denial for the revoked account. The control account should still be able to retrieve the document.

2. What this changes for Belgian and French companies

A small business can start with supplier folders and simulate a contractor leaving. A mid-sized company operating in Belgium and France can repeat the test when someone changes business units or teams. Have IT and the document owner agree on a maximum revocation delay based on document sensitivity and name the person who can suspend search across the collection.

3. Underside analysis of indexing and conversations

In a cloud, on-premises or hybrid deployment, check the entire retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline. Test search, citations and an already open conversation separately. If an excerpt reappears after the index denies access, examine conversation history and application caches. If the agent also queries Odoo Enterprise, use the same user profiles to verify that record-level business restrictions are enforced when it accesses the ERP.

4. Prepare to suspend search across the collection

If the delay exceeds the agreed limit, test suspending search across the collection and restoring it after checking permissions. Log the test document ID, timestamps and outcomes without copying the document into the logs. Repeat the scenario after changes to the connector, indexing or session management.

Before launch, agree on a maximum revocation delay and verify that the team can suspend search across the collection if that limit is exceeded.
