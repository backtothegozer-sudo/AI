Presight and Scale AI: evaluating critical agentic AI

Article created on 12 October 2026 · Publication analysed: 8 October 2026 · Source: Presight

Presight and Scale AI have signed a memorandum of understanding to explore agentic AI applications for UAE government and enterprise customers. The announcement puts evaluation, simulation, governance, and human authority in scope, without announcing a service that is already deployed.

1. An exploration framework, not a deployment

The official release describes work on reliable agentic applications, evaluation, simulation, AI governance, emergency response, and scenario modelling. Presight contributes sovereign AI capabilities and national-scale systems experience; Scale AI contributes evaluation, expert data, simulation, and agentic-workflow expertise.

This is an MOU, not a published architecture, production commitment, or residency guarantee. It would therefore be unsound to infer actual control of infrastructure, models, data, operators, or support from the announcement alone.

2. Evaluate before granting autonomy

The most useful signal is the role assigned to evaluation: define what an agent must achieve, which errors are unacceptable, and which decisions remain human, then verify those requirements in operation. For critical use cases, a general model benchmark is insufficient. Tests must include incomplete data, conflicting instructions, rejected actions, and return to a manual process.

Useful simulation also reproduces real dependencies: identities, tools, business interfaces, latency, supplier outage, and security incidents. It assesses the consequences of an action in a process, not just the textual quality of a response.

3. Sovereignty needs chain-by-chain evidence

A sovereign-AI claim does not remove the need to document data and backup jurisdiction, models and updates, the control plane, telemetry, privileged access, subprocessors, keys, and exit. These are separate proofs from an agent’s functional quality.

For a Belgian or French organisation, the same discipline applies in European cloud, private cloud, or on premises. Location is one parameter; effective control also relies on identities, flows, contracts, exportable logs, and the ability to stop or replace the service.

4. Operating frame for Odoo and ERP

In Odoo, an ERP, or a crisis-management tool, start with read, summary, and recommendation uses. Give each agent a dedicated identity, limit tools to one process, require approval for irreversible actions, and retain exportable traces. Simulations must verify that it cannot bypass segregation of duties, limits, or existing approvals.

Recommendation: before a critical agentic pilot, gather objectives, prohibited errors, test sets, tool permissions, logs, human controls, dependencies, and a shutdown procedure in an evidence pack. Measure reliability in operating conditions, not only in a demonstration.
