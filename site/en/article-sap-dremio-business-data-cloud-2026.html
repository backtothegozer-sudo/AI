SAP x Dremio: Business Data Cloud gets stronger for enterprise agentic AI

Article created on 10 May 2026 · Publication analyzed: 4 May 2026 · Source: SAP News Center

Share inLinkedIn XX fFacebook

In its official 4 May 2026 release, SAP said it plans to acquire Dremio to strengthen SAP Business Data Cloud. The signal is highly practical for enterprise AI: without a unified, governed, query-ready data layer, assistants and agents do not scale beyond pilot mode.

1. What SAP officially announced

SAP frames the move as a strategic extension of Business Data Cloud. The stated objective is to connect SAP and non-SAP data more effectively so analytics and AI scenarios can run on a more coherent and accessible semantic layer.

2. Why this matters for agentic AI

The hard problem for enterprise agents is not only model quality, but context quality. By strengthening governed access to distributed data, SAP is addressing a structural blocker: the ability for agents to reason over fresh, traceable, reconciled business data.

3. Operational reading for ERP and Odoo

For organizations operating across SAP, adjacent tools, and Odoo Belgium, Odoo France, or Odoo Enterprise flows, the message is direct: do not start by putting a new assistant everywhere. Start by hardening the data fabric that will make finance, supply-chain, procurement, and service AI use cases reliable.

In practical terms, catalog, access governance, lineage, and business semantics become prerequisites for generative and agentic AI. That is a direct execution topic for ERP modernization and for search demand around Odoo Enterprise automation, reporting, and operational performance.
