Submer: EUR 1B for a sovereign AI data center in Catalonia

Article created on 13 July 2026 · Publication analyzed: 11 July 2026 · Source: Submer

Share inLinkedIn XX fFacebook

Submer's official 11 July 2026 announcement adds a concrete building block to European sovereign AI: more than EUR 1 billion to turn the former Ercros site in Flix, Catalonia, into a sustainable AI data center developed and operated by Rubix Data Centers.

1. What is announced

Submer Group plans to invest more than EUR 1 billion in a next-generation AI data center in Flix. The project will be led by Rubix Data Centers, a Submer company, and is designed to provide the physical layer AI now depends on: power, land, cooling, buildings, and large-scale operations. The design highlights energy-efficient liquid cooling with no water consumption during normal operations.

2. Why this is a sovereign AI signal

Sovereign AI does not depend only on models or data residency. It also depends on Europe's ability to operate industrial sites that can host compute workloads, AI clouds, and inference platforms. The Flix project therefore connects local reindustrialization, infrastructure control, and compute capacity for European demand.

3. What organizations should verify

For Belgian and French companies, this type of announcement should make AI strategy more operational: where critical workloads will run, who operates the infrastructure, which service commitments cover availability, how data traceability is handled, and how energy cost is controlled. A credible sovereign AI project should document these dependencies before production rollout.

Map sensitive AI workloads against infrastructure criteria: location, operator, cooling, energy, reversibility, and supervision.
