AI agents: testing temporary access before production

Article created November 19, 2026 · Event analyzed November 18, 2026 · Source: Google Cloud

Google Cloud scheduled a November 18 webinar on delegating temporary privileges to AI agents. Its Privileged Access Manager (PAM) documentation gives enterprise teams a starting point for acceptance testing: verify who can obtain a role, for which task, for how long and with what evidence.

1. Define the access window

PAM lets administrators configure which identities can request specific roles, the maximum duration, a justification and approval where needed. PAM removes the roles it grants when the grant expires. For an agent, tie each request to an identified task and select the narrowest role that can perform it. Check any permissions the identity already holds outside PAM as well.

2. What changes for companies in Belgium and France

An SME can start with a read-only agent that reconciles documents in a supplier file. Corporate or public-sector IT teams can ask business and security teams to agree on which operations require approval. If the automation drafts a change in Odoo Enterprise, assign approval to a business owner and enforce write permissions in the ERP.

3. Underside analysis · separate retrieval from action

In a RAG system, enforce document permissions at retrieval time. For a business action proposed by the agent, separately verify the identity, target record and scope of the approval at the connector. In local, cloud or hybrid deployments, map where access decisions are made and where logs are retained. For a managed Mac fleet, also check whether authorization depends on device posture or on the identity of the service performing the action.

4. Test denial, expiry and retries

In staging, test a denied request, expiry during processing and a retry after a timeout. Verify that a queued task can no longer use expired access and that a retry cannot repeat a write already approved and recorded. Use a shared case identifier to reconcile cloud grant and expiry events with the approval and outcome recorded in the ERP.

Before allowing writes, ask the team to show with a test record that expired access is denied and that retries create no duplicates.
