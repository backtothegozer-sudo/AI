Apply AI Summit: turning a use case into testable acceptance

Article created November 19, 2026 · Event analysed November 17, 2026 · Source: European Commission

The Apply AI Summit, held in Brussels on November 17, concluded the first European AI Innovation Month. The European Commission brought together public-sector leaders, startups, businesses and researchers around applications in ten strategic sectors and the public sector. For an organisation, the practical issue is not to reproduce a demonstration: it is to prove that a use case produces a result within controlled boundaries.

1. Begin with a measurable business case

Select a limited task, such as preparing the qualification of a supplier request or proposing a document reconciliation. Before the pilot, set the turnaround time, expected quality, rate of cases needing rework, and accountable acceptance owner. An AI demonstration may inform a choice; it does not on its own measure the benefit in a live process.

2. Separate assistance from decision-making

For Odoo Belgium, Odoo France or Odoo Enterprise, start with authorised reading and a business-visible draft. Writes — creating third parties, changing an invoice or approving a payment — must remain subject to ERP rules, a dedicated identity and explicit approval. Document the connectors, accessible data and stop procedure before expanding the scope.

3. Test the expected case and denials

Build a set of fictional cases that includes incomplete requests, absent permissions and conflicting instructions. Check the quality of the proposal, but also the refusal of unauthorised access, the absence of writes without approval, and the ability to reconstruct the case from logs. Compare results with a manual baseline on the same sample.

4. Read the European signal without overpromising

The summit signals a European intention to accelerate sectoral adoption and showcase concrete projects. It does not certify a supplier, architecture or deployment’s compliance. Belgian and French organisations must therefore assess processing locations, administrator access, contracts, log retention and reversibility separately.

Before industrialising an AI use case, have the team validate its outcome metric, effective permissions, expected denials and recovery after an incident using a fictional case.
