Nscale x Microsoft in Portugal: Sines becomes a major anchor for sovereign AI in Europe

Article created on 6 May 2026 · Publication analyzed: 5 May 2026 · Source: Nscale Press Release

Share inLinkedIn XX fFacebook

In its official 5 May 2026 announcement, Nscale confirmed an expanded collaboration with Microsoft and Start Campus in Portugal, with more than 66,000 NVIDIA Rubin GPUs planned for the Sines site. The signal is direct: Europe is scaling frontier AI infrastructure without leaving its own regional footprint.

1. What is officially announced

Nscale says the expansion will add a second 200 MW building and additional shared infrastructure at Sines. The release frames this as a continuation of the first Microsoft deployment and positions the Portuguese campus as one of the largest AI infrastructure projects in Europe.

2. Why this matters for sovereign AI in Europe

The structural point is not only the GPU volume. It is the combination of compute capacity, European location, renewable energy, connectivity, and scaling runway. For organizations that need AI execution under regional governance constraints, Sines becomes a concrete option compared with fully extraterritorial architectures.

3. Operational reading for Odoo Belgium, Odoo France, and Odoo Enterprise

For Odoo Belgium, Odoo France, and Odoo Enterprise programs, the announcement strengthens the case for large-scale European execution tied to ERP data, documents, workflows, and internal assistants. That matters for semantic search, document intelligence, internal copilots, agentic automation, and other use cases that must balance performance, compliance, and reversibility.

The right response is not to focus only on available compute. Teams still need to define data sensitivity, inference location, access logs, evidence requirements, and how AI services connect into business workflows. That is where European infrastructure becomes an operational business asset rather than a simple hosting choice.
