SAP x IBM: Cloud ERP Private becomes a concrete signal for Odoo Belgium, Odoo France, and Odoo Enterprise

Article created on 3 July 2026 · Publication analyzed: 2 July 2026 · Source: SAP News

Share inLinkedIn XX fFacebook

On July 2, 2026, SAP and IBM announced that JYSK, GBM, DIFARE Group, and Plastilene Group had selected SAP Cloud ERP Private on IBM Power Virtual Server to modernize ERP workloads. The signal is concrete for AI Belgium, AI France, Odoo Belgium, Odoo France, and Odoo Enterprise: credible enterprise AI depends first on an ERP core that is resilient, secure, migratable, and ready for AI-enabled workflows.

1. What is actually being announced

This is not just a hosting update. SAP links ERP modernization, IBM Power infrastructure, hybrid cloud, and AI readiness in a single operating path. The message is that on-premises ERP customers need a structured route into SAP Cloud ERP Private with security, resilience, and scale control built in. IBM brings Power Virtual Server, cloud infrastructure, and a migration pattern that supports hybrid and multicloud environments.

2. Why it matters for enterprise AI

SAP also points to IBM research showing that companies embedding AI into ERP can achieve up to 27% higher ROI. The important point is not the number alone, but the sequence behind it: before agents, companies need a modern ERP foundation, reliable data, a usable data layer, and an operating platform that can absorb AI workflows without destabilizing the business core. For Odoo Enterprise, that is a direct reminder that profitable AI programs often start with application readiness, not with model selection alone.

3. The signal for Odoo Belgium and Odoo France

For Odoo Belgium and Odoo France, the competitive bar is moving toward the combination of ERP, private cloud, operational resilience, compliance, and AI execution. Companies no longer want only a functional ERP. They want a foundation that can support automation, copilots, document processing, and assisted decisions while preserving control over data, performance, and cost.

For Odoo Enterprise, the right benchmark is no longer feature coverage alone. The platform must also support AI scale-up with access control, hybrid architecture, observability, and a clear industrialization path.
